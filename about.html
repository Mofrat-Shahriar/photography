Biography

Hello, I'm
Mofrat Shahriar

I'm a self-taught photographer with a passion for finding quiet beauty in the world around me. What began as a casual hobby — a borrowed camera at a mountain trail — has grown into a dedicated creative practice that shapes how I see and move through life.

My photography spans landscapes that breathe, portraits that speak, wildlife moments that pause time, and macro worlds that expand the invisible. Each genre teaches me something different about patience, observation, and the precise moment when everything aligns.

Outside of photography, I'm drawn to travel, cinema, and long conversations about the way light behaves just before it disappears.

Specialisations

Photography Interests

The genres I live inside — each demanding a different kind of attention.

🏔

Landscape

Dawn climbs and twilight waits. I chase the moment when the world holds its breath — the precise colour temperature of first light on still water, or fog threading between mountain ridges.

🦅

Wildlife

Patience measured in hours. The discipline of wildlife photography has taught me more about stillness than any meditation. Every frame earned through presence and respect.

👤

Portrait

A portrait is a negotiation between the seen and the unseen. I work with available light to draw out something genuine — a moment of vulnerability that transforms into timeless art.

🔬

Macro

An alternate universe exists at the millimetre scale. Macro photography turns the ordinary — a petal, a raindrop, an insect eye — into alien landscapes of extraordinary detail.

🍽

Food

Food photography is still life with warmth. The challenge is translating flavour and texture into visual desire — making the viewer taste the image with their eyes.

✨

Miscellaneous

Some of the best photographs refuse to be categorised. Street moments, abstract light, architectural fragments — the images that surprise even me.

The Kit

Equipment & Gear

The tools I use to realise a vision — chosen for reliability, quality, and feel.

📷

Primary Camera

Sony A7 IV / Canon EOS R5 — full-frame mirrorless body offering exceptional dynamic range and low-light performance for all conditions.

🔭

Telephoto Lens

100–400mm f/4.5–5.6 GM — my reach for wildlife and compressed landscapes. Crisp across the zoom range with optical stabilisation.

🔍

Macro Lens

90mm f/2.8 Macro — a 1:1 ratio lens that opens an entire parallel world invisible to the naked eye.

🌄

Wide Angle Lens

16–35mm f/2.8 — the landscape workhorse. Ultra-wide perspective with edge-to-edge sharpness even wide open.

🎚

Post-Processing

Adobe Lightroom Classic & Photoshop — for RAW processing, colour grading, and the final cinematic look I'm known for.

📐

Support & Accessories

Carbon-fibre tripod, circular polariser filters, ND filter set, dual-battery grip, and weatherproof camera bag for all-terrain shooting.

The Journey

Photography Timeline

Key moments in my visual journey — from the first shutter click to today.

2018

The First Frame

Borrowed a friend's entry-level DSLR on a hiking trip. That single afternoon of chasing golden light through pine trees changed everything.

2019

First Camera

Saved up and bought my first mirrorless camera. Spent the year obsessively learning manual exposure, composition, and RAW processing.

2020

Macro Discovery

Locked indoors, I turned the camera on my immediate surroundings. A macro lens revealed an entire universe in my garden. My most transformative creative year.

2021

Wildlife Expeditions

First dedicated wildlife trip — three weeks following migratory birds across wetlands. Patience became my most important photographic skill.

2022–2023

Expanding the Portfolio

Formally explored portrait and food photography. Built this portfolio to document the breadth of what I love — one genre at a time.

2024–Present

Continuing the Journey

Still shooting, still learning. The camera remains the most honest tool I've found for paying attention to the world.
